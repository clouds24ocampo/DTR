# Roles and Permissions

The HRMS Dashboard uses a sophisticated role-based access system to ensure staff members have access to the tools they need while maintaining data security.

## Major Roles

### 1. Human Resources (HR)
- **Permissions**: Full access to Applicant Management, Employee Management, Job Management, and Payroll.
- **Key Tasks**: Hiring, performance reviews, document creation, and payroll processing.

### 2. Workforce Management
- **Permissions**: Access to DTR Tracking, Schedule Management, and Workplace Management.
- **Key Tasks**: Monitoring attendance, approving trips, and managing shift schedules.

### 3. Employee
- **Permissions**: Access to personal DTR, Leave requests, Payslips, and Profile.
- **Key Tasks**: Clocking in/out, applying for leave, and viewing personal documents.

### 4. Team Leader
- **Permissions**: Employee-level access plus oversight of their direct team's DTR and progress.
- **Key Tasks**: Reviewing team performance and approving minor requests.

### 5. Intern
- **Permissions**: Limited access focused on learning and task tracking.
- **Key Tasks**: Daily progress reporting and internal messaging.

## Permission Matrix (Overview)
- **Viewing**: Most users can view their own data. Management roles can view data across their department.
- **Editing**: Restricted to specific administrative roles (HR/Workforce).
- **Approving**: Only designated roles can approve leaves, trips, or payroll.

## Role Switching
Authorized users may be able to switch between roles (e.g., from HR to Employee view) using the "Switch Role" feature in the dashboard.
