# Getting Started for Staff

Welcome to the HRMS Dashboard! This guide will help you get set up and start using the system effectively.

## Step 1: Logging In
- Access the dashboard at the provided URL.
- Enter your employee credentials.
- If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

## Step 2: Dashboard Overview
- Your landing page will be customized based on your role (HR, Employee, etc.).
- Use the sidebar to navigate between different modules like DTR, Leave, and Messages.
- Check the top header for notifications and your profile menu.

## Step 3: Daily Tasks (Attendance)
- **Clocking In**: Navigate to the DTR page and click "Clock In". Ensure your location services are enabled if required.
- **Breaks**: Use the break buttons to track your rest periods throughout the day.
- **Clocking Out**: Don't forget to click "Clock Out" before leaving for the day.

## Step 4: Managing Your Profile
- Keep your contact information and documents up to date in the "Profile" section.
- View your payslips and leave balance directly from your personal dashboard.

## Step 5: Communication
- Use the "Messages" module for real-time chat with your colleagues.
- Keep an eye on the notification bell for important announcements.

## Need Support?
If you encounter any issues, use the built-in ChatBot or contact the HR/IT support team through the "Support" channel.
